- Skills: Perception +3
- Senses: Passive Perception 13
- Languages: Common, and any other relevant languages for the setting.

- Challenge: 1 (200 XP)
- Proficiency Bonus: +2

Baron-Sir Caius hires personal retainers in addition to the bounty hunters and mercenaries that make up much of his staff.
These people are the cream of the crop, with the best pay that comes directly from the hand of the Baron-Sir himself.

## Traits

*Shielded.* If the Baron's Guard is wielding a shield, it's armour class increases by 2 (giving it a total armour class of 19).

*Combat Training.* The Baron's Guard is trained in one of three combat styles.
Each combat style provides certain benefits.

- Bodyguard: Enables the *Bait and Switch* ability.
- Enforcer: Enables the *Heavy Hitter* ability.
- Battlemage: The Baron's Guard gains the spellcasting features described below.

*Bait and Switch.* As part of it's movement, the Baron's Guard switches places with an adjacent ally, granting the ally an additional 4 to its armour class so long as it remains adjacent to the Baron's Guard.
The Baron's Guard may use this ability as a reaction to an adjacent ally taking damage, potentially causing a hit to miss.
If taken as a reaction, the ally gains the prone condition, regardless of whether it took damage from the attack.

*Heavy Hitter.* When the Baron's Guard hits a creature with a melee attack, the creature must succeed in a contested Strength (Athletics) check against the Baron's Guard or fall prone.
The Baron's Guard may immediately make an additional melee attack against a creature knocked down in this way as a bonus action.

## Actions

The bear makes one attack, either with it's bite or with it's claws.

**Weapon Strike**: *Melee weapon attack*, +6 to hit, reach 5ft., 1 target. Hit; 8 (1d8 + 4) piercing damage.

## Bonus Action

**Second Wind**: 1/day. The Baron's Guard regains 10 (1d10+4) hit points.

## Spellcasting

A battlemage Baron's Guard gains access to the spellcasting features below.
Intelligence is its spellcasting ability, it has a spell attack modifier of +4, and spell save DC of 12.

Level 1 Spells (2/day):

- Longstrider
- Jump
- Shield
