# Feywild Horsewhistle

Uncommon magic item (ring).

A wooden ring whose interior side is covered in warm, comfortable vines that expand and contract to keep the ring stable on the wearer's hand.
The pattern on the outside of the ring resembles the flowing mane of a horse, and where a gem would usually be placed inside an inset there is instead a small carved horse's head protruding from the ring.

The ring initially only has the [Nature's Ally](#natures-ally) property.
Once the balance of Trawidok is restored, it gains the [Horsewhistler](#horsewhistler) and [Swift Passage](#swift-passage) properties.

## Nature's Ally

If the balance of Trawidok has not been restored, any pegasid or Trawidok-native horses recognise the wearer as an ally if they do nothing nefarious to exacerbate the imbalance.
The wearer of the ring is also aware of the location and emotional state of any such creatures within 120ft that regard them in this way.

The wearer can use a bonus action on each of their turns to issue a telepathic command to one such creature they are aware of, directing the creature on how to act on its next turn in combat.
The target creature will only act in ways that it deems will work to restore the balance of Trawidok.

## Horsewhistler

The wearer of the ring can communicate with Equine creatures as if under the effects of the Speak with Animals spell, so long as they wear the ring.
Removing the ring causes the wearer to loose this effect.

## Swift Passage

The wearer can cast the Misty Step spell without expending a spell slot.
Once used in this way, the ring cannot use this property until after the next dawn.

When Misty Step is in using this property, spectral pegasid and horses appear to sweep the wearer up onto their backs and transport them to the target destination, before dissipating.
