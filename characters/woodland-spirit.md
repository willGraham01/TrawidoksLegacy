## At a Glance

- Race: Fey
- Age: A few years younger than Trawidok forest
- Gender: Nonbinary (it/they)
- Occupation: Guardian of Trawidok Forest

## Backstory

The Spirit came into being shortly after Trawidok forest matured.
A manifestation of the forest's will, it exists to preserve the balance between nature and civilisation in the Trawidok area.

It was the Spirit that made the original pact with the town of Trawidok, beginning its arrangement with the village which eventually evolved into the Rite of Passage.
The Spirit encourages its subordinates to undertake this Rite, to ensure they maintain a sense of understanding of the needs of the people that rely on the forest to live, whilst also being able to exert influence over how the surrounding natural world is moulded by the villagers.

The Spirit has sensed an imbalance since the Baron-Sir took over the racecourse.
It knows that some of its subordinates who undertook the Rite are now trapped and held by the Baron-Sir, which it sees as an affront to the purpose of the Rite and arrangement with the village - an affront it seeks to correct.
It has had Trohet lead the operational aspects of the attempts to free its trapped kin, though has been involved in the plans that have been put in motion.

## Appearance & Personality

The Woodland Spirit takes on the appearance of a unicorn.

It is dedicated to upholding the balance between nature and civilisation in the area surrounding Trawidok forest.
It also possesses a rather novel perspective on this matter compared to other fey, actively inviting it's subordinates to partake in the Trawidok Rite of Passage to better understand the needs and desires of the people of Trawidok, which it feels will help sustain the balance it seeks to maintain.

The Spirit wants to see balance returned to the Trawidok area, which means the freedom of any Trawidok horses that are currently trapped by the Baron-Sir.
It is also reluctant to continue sending its subordinates on the Rite, slightly afraid they will end up like their trapped kin.

## Relations

- Trohet: A subordinate of the Spirit, who is now its right-hand-man (horse?) leading attempts to free the other trapped Trawidok horses.
- Pakana: A subordinate of the Spirit, and the last fey to partake in the Rite. Allowed to bond with Zanke both upon Trohet's recommendation from knowing Ginter, and the Spirit's need for another pair of "eyes" that Trohet can communicate with in the village.

## Plot Relevant Knowledge

- The Spirit knows the true version of events behind the legend of Trawidok.
- The Spirit can also explain the unusual behaviour of the Trawidok-native horses, in light of knowing they are actually fey.
  - Such fey give up their powers during the Rite, to take on the form of a (slightly superior) horse and to live alongside a member of the village.
  - The experience ensures that the fey do not fall out of touch with the needs of the village, but also ensures they have some control over how the village expands and interacts with the surrounding forest.
  - It will also explain why non-Trawidok native horses are unaffected by the seemingly bizarre goings-on in the village.
- If asked for it's name, it will call itself "Wind Between the Trees".

## Statblock

The Woodland Spirit uses a modified version of the Unicorn statblock.

- Armour Class: 12
- Hit Points: 97 (13d10+26)
- Speed: 50ft., fly 90ft.

|   STR   |   DEX   |   CON   |   INT   |   WIS   |   CHA   |
|:-------:|:-------:|:-------:|:-------:|:-------:|:-------:|
| 18 (+4) | 14 (+2) | 15 (+2) | 11 (+0) | 17 (+3) | 16 (+3) |

- Saving Throws: Wis +5, Cha +5
- Senses: Darkvision 60ft., Passive Perception 13
- Languages: Celestial, Elvish, Sylvan; telepathy 120ft.
- Immunities: Poison
- Condition Immunities: Poisoned, Charmed, Paralysed

**Legendary Resistance (3/Day)**. If the unicorn fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

**Magic Resistance**. The unicorn has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

### Actions in Combat

- **Multiattack**. The unicorn makes one Hooves attack and one Radiant Horn attack.
- **Hooves**. _Melee Weapon Attack_: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
  _On hit_: 11 (2d6 + 4) Bludgeoning damage.
- **Radiant Horn**. _Melee Weapon Attack_: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
  _On hit_: 10 (1d10 + 4) Radiant damage.

### Spellcasting

As an action, the unicorn casts one of the following spells, requiring no spell components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 14):

At Will:

- Detect Evil and Good
- Druidcraft

1/Day Each:

- Calm Emotions
- Dispel Evil and Good
- Entangle
- Pass without Trace
- Word of Recall
