## At a Glance

- Race: Fey Spirit
- Age: 50
- Gender: Male (he/him)
- Occupation: Native Trawidok horse, formerly [Ginter Dijkstra](FIXME)'s horse-partner.

## Backstory

Trohet is a fey spirit that partook in Ginter's Rite of Passage, becoming his horse-partner.
His time with Ginter was largely spent working the Dijkstra farm, and participating in training lessons for the children of the village (notably Zanke).
He also would have overheard many conversations between Hierim and Ginter when hitched outside one of their houses, as well as between the Dijkstra family.

Trohet was sympathetic to Ginter's plan to sabotage the [Baron-Sir](./caius-equirrion-younger.md)'s setup.
When it resulted in failure, he accepted Ginter's wishes for him to be free and return to the forest, his loyalty clashing somewhat with Ginter's wishes and knowing that returning to the forest was the best way to work towards restoring Trawidok's balance.
He has since acted as something of a scout for the Woodland Spirit, and coordinated the breakout of several Trawidok horses at the racecourse prior to the events of the story.

## Appearance & Personality

Trohet usually appears in a form without his wings, only displaying them when the party encounters him in [the Spirit's Grove](../scenes/07-spirits-grove.md).
When in this form, he appears to be a large black horse, bearing a distinctive 3-white-sock marking on all but his front right leg.
His mane is long and consists of raven-black hair.

When his wings are visible, these are also black-feathered.

Trohet is a fey spirit of Trawidok forest, who willingly participated in the Rite of Passage to better understand the people of Trawidok.
He is loyal to the [Woodland Spirit](FIXME) and preserving the balance of nature around Trawidok.
Aside from these traits, he also bears the typical character traits of fey creatures - mischievous at times, but this has been tempered by his experiences working alongside Ginter.
As per the nature of the Rite, Trohet's personality is mostly mirrored by that of Ginter - a deep respect for tradition, preserving knowledge and customs, and passing these on to the youth or future generations.

## Relations

- Ginter Dijkstra: Trohet's partner met during the Rite of Passage. Ginter released Trohet before his execution to prevent Trohet becoming a captive to the [Baron-Sir](./caius-equirrion-younger.md).
- Zanke Dijkstra: Trohet knows Zanke from childhood, and was the horse that she learnt to ride on.
  He is largely friendly towards her, though has not actually interacted with her since his release.
- Woodland Spirit: Trohet is fiercely loyal to the Woodland Spirit and its ideals.

## Plot Relevant Knowledge

Trohet knows the same information as the Woodland Spirit, though he will often defer questions to the Sprit if he is asked directly.
His perspective on the information will also be framed slightly differently, but with the overall same views and opinions.
