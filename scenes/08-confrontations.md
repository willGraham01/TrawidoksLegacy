The party returns to Trawidok either for their reward, or to confront the Baron-Sir on the Spirit's wishes.

This scene serves to do the following things:

- Resolves the story and fate of Trawidok.
- Rewards the party for the adventure.

At the end of the scene, the party should hopefully:

- Have a complete understanding of the events of the adventure.
- Have resolved the situation in Trawidok.

## Setting

This scene takes place in: Trawidok.

Depending on how the party has gone about the adventure (and the events [in the Grove](./07-spirits-grove.md)), the party may return to Trawidok in one of three states:

- If the party sided with the Baron-Sir in the grove and killed the Woodland Spirit (and recovered the horses), they can just return to the racecourse and get their reward.
  [Use the "Reward from the Baron" event](#reward-from-the-baron).
- If the party sided with the Woodland Spirit, and the Baron-Sir is unaware that they are working against him, then they will need to proceed to free the one remaining Trawidok horse from the racecourse.
  [Use the "Free the Horses!" event](#free-the-horses).
- If the party sided with the Woodland Spirit, and the Baron-Sir is aware that someone is working against him, then the party's attempts to free the remaining horses will be conflated with the Baron-Sir's own actions.
  [Use the "Lynching" event](#lynching).

## Lynching

In this scenario, the party returns to Trawidok having sided with the Woodland Spirit, intending to free the remaining "trapped" horses and restore the balance of nature.
However, the Baron-Sir is also aware that people are working against him (even if he does not know who it is).

The party will return to Trawidok and see a commotion at the racecourse grounds.
A large crowd of townsfolk will be gathered on the course grounds, where a gallows has been hastily assembled.
The Baron-Sir and his personal guards are on the gallows stage itself, with the Baron-Sir seemingly addressing the crowd.
Rolf and any surviving members of his band will be acting as bouncers to the crowd, though they themselves will be quite nervous and unsure of the course of action that the Baron-Sir is taking.
The crowd consists of citizens of Trawidok; a mixture of both the traditionalists (who look visibly angry and agitated), and the younger generation (who look scared and nervous).
Some of the nobility will also be present, but inside the viewing gallery of the racecourse concourse - the Baron-Sir will be addressing them with the "culprit" who stole their horses.

Depending on who the Baron-Sir suspects of working against him, the person being hung changes.

- The Baron-Sir suspects Hierim, if the party stole the ledger and failed to return it, or shared its information with Hierim and didn't discourage him from pursuing leads from the information.
- The Baron-Sir suspects Zanke otherwise.

Whoever of the two is *not* being hung will be present in the crowd, and will provide the party with a run-down of the situation when they reach the scene.
If Zanke is being hung, Pakana has been moved to an enclosure in the stables, along with any other Trawidok horses yet to be freed.
If she is not being hung, he will be by her side in the crowd.
Other notable villagers may also be in the crowd, such as Malon and Teodor.

Regardless, the Baron-Sir will be speaking to the crowd / nobles in the concourse, claiming to have found the culprit.
The party can listen to the speech, prepare their actions whilst the Baron-Sir is speaking, or make themselves known in an attempt to detour proceedings.
If left uninterrupted, the Baron-Sir will finish his speech and release the gallows' trapdoor, beginning the hanging as per the [combat encounter](#combat-encounter-lynching) below.

Preparations the party can undertake may include:

- If the party are on cordial terms with Rolf, characters may notice that him and his band are uneasy with the entire setup.
  - (Persuasion DC 13, ADV: The party is on good terms with Rolf, or makes a suitable appeal to his character) To convince Rolf that he doesn't want to be a part of this.
    Rolf will agree not to interfere with whatever the party is planning next.
    - Note that to make this check *and* not give the game away to the Baron-Sir or one of his guards, the character interacting with Rolf will need to be subtle in how they push to the front of the crowd and engage him in conversation.
  - (Insight DC 13, ADV: The character has a good understanding of Rolf's character) To convince Rolf that he doesn't want to be a part of this.
    Rolf will agree not to interfere with whatever the party is planning next.
    - Note that Rolf and his band are mercenaries and bounty hunters, so are somewhat ruthless but ultimately hold themselves accountable to some legal system.
      A character may leverage this viewpoint when attempting to convince Rolf.
- A character with the [Feywild Horsewhistle](../items/feywild-horsewhistle.md) will be able to sense the locations of Pakana and the other Trawidok horses.
  They can prepare the horses with some actions to take when things kick off.
  - Horses that are in the stables can be commanded to attempt to break out, as per the notes in the [corresponding combat](#combat-encounter-lynching).
- The party can reveal themselves to the Baron-Sir and his guards, and engage him in public conversation.
  The Baron-Sir won't have his mind changed, but the party may be able to rally some support from the crowd in the upcoming encounter.
  He will also threaten to arrest the party if they interfere, or if he puts the context clues together and deduces the party's involvement, will order Rolf to arrest the party.
  If Rolf is already on-side with the party, the Baron-Sir will really loose his temper and [order the hanging to begin](#combat-encounter-lynching), and his guards to apprehend both the party and Rolf (for not following paid orders).

Eventually, the hanging will begin and the [combat encounter](#combat-encounter-lynching) will start.
Once combat is resolved, the fallout is handled [in the conclusion](#conclusion).

## Conclusion

## Combat Encounter (Lynching)

This combat encounter begins when the Baron-Sir calls for the hanging to start, or the party takes an action which interrupts the Baron-Sir's intended proceedings.
At the start of the combat, the Baron-Sir will release the trapdoor on the gallows, which causes whoever of Hierim or Zanke is there to start suffocating (see [the notes](#notes)).

### Encounter Information

Trigger: The Baron-Sir's proceedings are interrupted, or he orders the hanging to begin.
Intended difficulty: Hard

Participants based on party size:

| Party size | Baron-Sir Caius | Baron's Guard | Other adjustments |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|-|
| 3- | 1 | 2 | Rolf's band, and the crowd may also be present and take sides |
| 4  | 1 | 3 | Rolf's band, and the crowd may also be present and take sides |
| 5  | 1 | 3 | Rolf's band, and the crowd may also be present and take sides |
| 6+ | 1 | 4 | Rolf's band, and the crowd may also be present and take sides |

Enemy Objectives:

- Ensure the hanging takes place.
- Apprehend or kill the party and other troublemakers in the crowd.

### Initial Locations

The Baron-Sir and his personal guards start on the gallows stage.

Rolf and his band start on the ground level between the stage and the crowd.

The party is likely to be either within the same general vicinity as the crowd, or may have positioned themselves advantageously in their preparation.

### Other Encounter Factors

If the crowd was riled up, they can occupy Rolf's band for the party.
If Rolf was convinced to stand down, a few brave members of the crowd will attempt to storm the stage and free the victim who is hanging.
The combat utility of the people in the crowd is limited (use the commoner stat block) so they will focus on rescuing their own rather than fighting and attacking professional soldiers.

Any Trawidok horses that are trapped in the stables can be commanded to attempt to break out of the stables, via the wooden wall.
They can make attacks against the stable wall, and are required to do 20 points of damage to it before it cracks open - allies outside the stables can also contribute damage to these efforts too.
Once released, the horses will attempt to make a break for the forest *unless* otherwise commanded by the wearer of the [Feywild Horsewhistle](../items/feywild-horsewhistle.md).

### Notes

At the start of every round, the victim who is hanging must make a DC 15 constitution saving throw.
On a failure, they gain a suffocation point.
Once they acquire more suffocation points than the maximum of their constitution modifier or 1, they die.

Suffocation points are immediately lost if the victim is freed from suspension from the gallows.
For example; by closing the trapdoor again (allowing them to stand), another character holding them up in the air, or cutting the suspending rope.
If they are suspended again, they will have to restart making constitution saves each round.
